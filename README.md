# Fog Harvesting Research

## Project Overview

This research project explores how fog harvesting can be used as a method of collecting water from atmospheric moisture. Through exploring fog harvesting design changes, water collection can be maximized

Fog harvesting is a passive water collection technique that uses surfaces or mesh structures to capture suspended water droplets. The collected droplets accumulate, combine, and flow into a collection system.

This research explores the engineering principles behind fog harvesting and its potential as a sustainable water collection technology.

## Research Objectives

- Investigate the scientific principles behind fog collection.
- Explore the factors that influence water collection efficiency.
- Examine fog harvesting as a potential sustainable water resource.
- Evaluate engineering considerations for designing effective fog collection systems.

## Engineering Concepts

**Materials Science:** Investigating how surface properties and mesh materials influence droplet capture.

**Environmental Engineering:** Exploring alternative water collection methods for regions experiencing water scarcity.

**Sustainable Design:** Considering energy-efficient approaches to water collection.
